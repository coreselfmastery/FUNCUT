# Niche playbooks (faceless)

One look per niche, ready to plan against. Each playbook: the look, the
sound, the pacing, the queries, and three hook angles. Combine with the
generic rules (SKILL.md §3); when two rules collide, the niche wins.

## History
- **Look:** archive texture — `super-8`/`polaroid` on period shots only, or
  `noir` for pre-1950s. Motion: `ken-burns-in` on photographs, slow.
  Captions: serif-adjacent presets (editorial/notes), cream on dark.
- **Sound:** FreePD "scoring documentary tension"; Foley (paper, pages),
  Ambience (wind, crowds, fire). SFX budget low — cuts speak.
- **Pacing:** calm, 6–10 cpm, 45–90 s. Transitions: `dissolve`,
  `clock-wipe`, one `iris` reveal per artifact.
- **Queries:** "ancient ruins", "old map parchment close-up", "candlelit
  archive", "marble statue slow pan", "battlefield wide".
- **Hooks:** the anachronism ("This map is 500 years wrong — on purpose") ·
  the price ("This letter cost a fortune to send") · the eyewitness ("He
  wrote this the night before the end").

## Tech / AI
- **Look:** `cool` blues, `vaporwave`/`cyberpunk` for retro beats; text
  presets tech/code/pixel. Motion: `slide-left` on code, `punch-zoom` on
  product reveals. Captions mono-clean.
- **Sound:** synth pulse; UI clicks on text pops; Digital on glitches.
  Transitions: `whip-pan`, `push`, `rgb-split-wipe`, `glitch-transition`.
- **Pacing:** energetic, 16–24 cpm, 30–45 s.
- **Queries:** "circuit board macro", "server room lights", "code on screen
  close-up", "robot arm assembly", "city aerial night".
- **Hooks:** the taboo ("The chip nobody is allowed to photograph") · the
  number ("This phone has more memory than every Apollo mission combined") ·
  the reversal ("AI didn't take the job — it took the boring half").

## Luxury / "dark psychology" adjacent
- **Look:** `teal-orange` moderate or `warm` candlelight; text
  editorial/fashion/hairline, gold accent (#d4af37 family); `tracking
  expand` title cards. Slow everything.
- **Sound:** elegant strings; `Whoosh` soft only; `light-leak-flash` on
  sparkle beats. SFX sparse.
- **Pacing:** calm-standard, 8–12 cpm, 30–60 s.
- **Queries:** "gold texture macro", "watch mechanism close-up", "private
  jet interior", "yacht aerial sunset", "champagne pour slow motion".
- **Hooks:** the price of nothing ("This room costs more per night than a
  car") · the secret rule ("Luxury hotels never put this in the room") ·
  the craft ("It takes 200 hours to make what's in this box").

## Motivation / discipline
- **Look:** `warm` sunrise or `bleach-bypass` gym grit; text
  impact/poster; accent red or white. Punch zooms on verbs.
- **Sound:** "epic inspiring build"; Risers into Impacts; heartbeat-adjacent
  under quiet lines.
- **Pacing:** energetic, 16–24 cpm, 30–45 s.
- **Queries:** "runner sunrise road", "climber summit reach", "boxer heavy
  bag sweat", "storm clouds timelapse", "swimmer pool lanes dawn".
- **Hooks:** the debt ("Nobody warns you about day 200") · the micro-rule
  ("The 5-minute rule that fixes mornings") · the identity flip ("You don't
  need motivation. You need this instead").

## Finance / money
- **Look:** `cool`/`bleach-bypass`; text clean/impact, green/red accents
  (#26a69a/#ef5350 family); numbers ALWAYS get their own card.
- **Sound:** tight pulse; Notification dings on stats; Keyboard on
  spreadsheet beats.
- **Pacing:** energetic, 16–24 cpm, 30–60 s.
- **Queries:** "stock chart close-up", "coins stacking macro", "printing
  press money", "skyline financial district", "port containers aerial".
- **Hooks:** the reframe ("Your salary is priced like milk. Here's the
  difference") · the trap ("This fee eats $47,000 of your retirement —
  quietly") · the scale ("$1 at 22 is $17 at 62. The math nobody shows
  you").

## Science / nature
- **Look:** clean — `teal-orange` light or none; captions bold-clean
  (bold-caption), accent cyan (#7fd8ff family). Motion `ken-burns-*` for
  wonder, `punch-zoom` on the number.
- **Sound:** "ambient wonder"; Risers into reveals; Ambience beds per world.
- **Pacing:** standard, 10–16 cpm, 30–60 s.
- **Queries:** "microscope slide macro", "space nebula", "laboratory
  pipette", "ocean underwater wide", "timelapse flower bloom".
- **Hooks:** the invisible ("You swallow a living machine every day") ·
  the scale ("This drop holds more life than every zoo on Earth") · the
  misunderstanding ("Salt isn't FROM the sea. It's from mountains").

## Horror / mystery
- **Look:** desaturated/mono family; text mystery/stencil; red accent used
  ONCE. Slow drifts, `static` holds. Stills + dark ambience beat footage.
- **Sound:** "dark drone tension"; smash cuts with silence; NO music in the
  build — room tone only.
- **Pacing:** calm-until-the-sting, 6–10 cpm then ONE smash cut at 70–80 %
  runtime; 60–120 s.
- **Queries:** "dark forest night fog", "abandoned corridor flashlight",
  "empty street lamp flicker", "crows on branch", "old house window night".
- **Hooks:** the ordinary gone wrong ("This house has been empty for 40
  years. The lights still come on") · the document ("The tape cuts here
  every time") · the rule ("Never answer the third knock").

## Fitness / health
- **Look:** `bleach-bypass` or hot `warm`; text impact; motion
  punch-zoom on reps. Overlay text cards with plates for clarity.
- **Sound:** "workout electronic drive"; Impact on every rep cut; heartbeat
  for stats.
- **Pacing:** energetic-hype, 20–30 cpm, 30–45 s.
- **Queries:** "gym barbell plates chalk", "meal prep overhead", "treadmill
  close-up feet", "jump rope shadow", "protein shake pour slow motion".
- **Hooks:** the myth ("Eating at night doesn't do what you think") · the
  math ("This is what 300 extra calories a day looks like in a year") ·
  the swap ("Replace 10 minutes of this with this").

## Gaming
- **Look:** `vaporwave`/`cyberpunk`/`vhs` CRT; text pixel/comic; RGB accent
  (#ff006e family). Transitions: glitch family, spin, cube.
- **Sound:** synthwave; UI clicks; Digital glitches on cuts.
- **Pacing:** hype, 24–40 cpm, 20–35 s.
- **Queries:** "arcade neon lights", "esports arena crowd", "controller
  hands glow", "retro console close-up", "racing game sim rig".
- **Hooks:** the record ("This speedrun was considered impossible for 9
  years") · the economics ("This skin sells for more than a console") ·
  the trivia ("The most-played game ever was a mistake").

## How-to / explainer
- **Look:** none/light `warm`; THE OBJECT is the visual — overhead desk
  shots, macro on hands. Text friendly/marker; one accent color. Motion:
  slide + static; the steps are the rhythm.
- **Sound:** "ukulele light"/"clean background"; Foley on every physical
  action (paper, clicks, pours); Keyboard for tutorial beats.
- **Pacing:** standard, 10–16 cpm, 30–90 s.
- **Queries:** the literal object + action ("pouring resin mold close-up",
  "hands folding origami crane overhead", "paint roller wall stroke").
- **Hooks:** the failure ("You're doing this right — that's why it looks
  wrong") · the shortcut ("3 steps, not 12") · the finish-first ("This is
  what you're making. Here's the only hard part").
