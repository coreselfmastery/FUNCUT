At audit time, main was `9a6d7874` (the #220 merge), with PRs #221 and
#222 in flight. Five deep passes were made over the tree - the one-click spine,
the model inventory and lifecycle, the signal extraction and consumption
map, the AUTO EDIT button's UI path, and the stock-footage intelligence -
and every finding below was read out of the code, not taken from a
document. This file is the working list the next sessions cut from. PR #222
is still open; its proposed changes are not counted as landed on main until
its current checks pass and it merges.

The one sentence verdict: **the machine is full of intelligence the button
does not spend.** The extraction layers are real and measured - 70 voice
readings, 38 visual actions behind a 30-model shelf, a CLAP/YAMNet reader
over a 530-sound shelf, four picture models - but the one-click spine
consumes a fraction of each, three whole sense doors are never called by
it, the B-roll and music shopping is keyword metadata where embeddings
exist, and the headless path runs its fan stages one after another where
the window runs them side by side.

## The 50-item TODO

Priority P0 is "the button spends everything it has"; P1 is "the models
are whole, safe and reachable"; P2 is "the window tells the truth while
it runs"; P3 is "the guards cannot be satisfied by prose".

## What is deliberately NOT on the list

- **OpenVid stays out of AUTO EDIT.** It has no per-clip licence; the ban
  (`director_program.rs:73-75`) is correct and stays.
- **No TTS inside AUTO EDIT.** The button edits narration that exists;
  generating a voice is the Director's and the studio's job. If a user
  wants script-to-video in one press, that is a new stage behind an
  explicit argument, not a silent behaviour change.
- **The default deliverable stays the timeline.** No export without the
  user's click; the acceptance gates that ride an encoded file stay
  honestly `Skipped` in timeline mode.
- **The degradation contracts stay as they are.** An absent generator
  answers `ok: true, reason: …`; a failed shelf search leaves the slot
  empty rather than padding with a bad match; a refused licence is
  recorded, never laundered. Every item above must keep those promises.
- **Geneva is not replaced.** The sidecar's limits are documented limits,
  not defects to route around.
