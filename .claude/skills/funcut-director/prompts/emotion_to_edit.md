# Emotion to edit — the seven-label recipe

The emotion read is not a mood word on a card. Each label is a bundle of
decisions: pace, cut type, caption behaviour, sound, grade, motion, duck and
what to avoid. Intensity scales the recipe; it never changes the label.

| Label | Pace | Cut types | Captions | Sound | Grade | Motion | Duck | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **joy** | +10–20%, lift at the turn | `match`, `action`, `beat` | bright accent, one exclamation at most | major-key bed, light Foley, airy transitions | warm, lifted, low contrast | soft pop, upward drift, generous hold | −9 | irony, cold teal, smash on warmth |
| **sadness** | −10–20%, let the take breathe | `cutaway`, `l`, hold | plain, slow reveal, no bouncing | low drone, piano, room tone, sparse | cool, desaturated, soft roll-off | slow fade, no bounce, long settle | −15 | whip pans, montage, comic SFX |
| **anger** | +15–25%, hard turns | `smash`, `jump`, `contrast` | short, punchy, stressed word in caps | impact, distorted bass, silence before the hit | red/orange, crushed blacks, high contrast | snap-in, punch, no float | −9 | soft dissolve, whimsical music |
| **fear** | −5–15%, then a spike | `jump`, `cutaway`, `contrast` | fragmented, withheld information | sub drone, riser, breath, no melody | cold, crushed, low-key | handheld drift, slow push, sudden snap | −15 | bright major bed, cute type |
| **surprise** | spike then settle | `freeze`, `flash`, `smash`, `whip` | one word, big, then the explanation | stinger, reverse, impact, then air | neutral with one colour accent | punch zoom, freeze, fast settle | −12 | repeating the trick every section |
| **disgust** | −5–10%, uncomfortable hold | `insert`, `cutaway`, `contrast` | close, clinical, no emphasis | texture, low noise, scrape, no melody | sickly green/yellow, high mid contrast | slow reveal, macro drift | −15 | clean corporate gloss |
| **neutral** | standard | `hard`, `match`, `insert` | measured, 2–4 words | bed at the act's level, clean Foley | balanced, one look | restrained, purposeful | −12 | over-designing a calm line |

## How intensity spends

- **0.0–0.35:** use the label's pace and grade only; no signature cut.
- **0.35–0.65:** add one cut type from the row and one sound choice.
- **0.65–1.0:** the full row; the cut type can carry the section, and the
  grade/motion can push harder.

## How acts combine with labels

The act says **where** the beat sits; the label says **how** it behaves:

- `hook` + fear/anger: start tight, cut early, withhold the wide.
- `setup` + neutral/sadness: establish, breathe, one clear image.
- `tension` + fear/anger: shorten, repeat a motif, raise the duck.
- `turn` + surprise/joy: change the cut type and the grade at the same seam.
- `reveal` + joy/awe: open the frame, lift the bed, let the motion settle.
- `proof` + neutral: evidence, inserts, numbers, no decoration.
- `cta` + joy/resolve: one ask, one look, one final image.

If the label and the act disagree, the **act wins the structure** and the
label wins the texture. Log both in `signalLog`; never average them into a
mood that drives nothing.
